# Emo

Emo maintains bonds, supports rift, and enables the tank and MeMo energy setup.

## Build

[Emo;OgNDwcPPT3MaR1CkE0lxDHEyD]

## Gear

Use the shared [Emo gear](/tactics/gear/emo) page as the baseline.

## Notes

### Gloom

- Use [Ebon Escape] over the hill, then go to the wave 1 spot.
- Wait for the tank to [Recall] out and use [Balthazar's Spirit] on him.
- Tank wave 3 and wave 8.
- Provide a [Recall] target for the tank, fully bond him, then go to rift with MeMo.

### Foundry

- Ping for the third snake.
- Take the quest to spawn the Fury.
- Tank might ask you to remove [Protective Bond] to help him spike the Fury.

### City

- Maintain bonds on the team.

### Veil
- Tank melee waves during 360. [Infuse health] the tank on fiends waves (fuse pull).
- Maintain bond range on the tank for ranger hill.
- Pull the front pop of mesmer trench and [Ebon escape] to the necro.
- If the tank managed to pull the left-side clover group to the glitch spot, you will be the clover tank.
- Keep range for TK on the ele lord.
- Tank 3-3 with ranger.

# Fundamentals

These concepts apply to every run. Learn them once, then focus on what changes per strategy.

## Communication and calls

- Keep calls short in critical moments.
- Call beforehand if you need more time to get your cooldowns before a spike or notice something wrong. (eg. You have been the target of [Power block], missing [Edge of extinction], [Ether renewal] has been stripped)
- Call shared targets to avoid overlaps with other spikers. (eg. which despair or which dreamy you [Mistrust] in foundry, which rage or dryder you use [Pain Inverter] on)
- Call when you're overwhelmed (eg. unwanted aggros, hard to cover off-damage) so the team can help you out.

## Cast range

Cast range is the distance you can cast a spell from your target.
If your distance between you and your target is greater than your cast range, the game will automatically move you closer to the target - more than what's necessary for the spell to be cast.
The target will then be able to see you before you can cast the spell.

This concept is especially important when targets have spells that can interrupt you, such as [Gale] or [Cry of frustration] in city.
Instead of letting this happen, move manually to the target until your aggro range is just about to touch the target's yellow outline.
Below, you can see on the left a minimap with aggro range only and the ideal distance, and on the right the [GWToolbox](https://www.gwtoolbox.com) minimap which shows the cast range.

This fundamental is absolutely critical to get right when spiking the Fury in foundry as Komalie guardians have loads of interrupts such as [Power drain], [Power spike] and [Cry of frustration].

## Aggro management

- Understand how [Share Aggro](/tactics/glossary/#share-aggro), [Bad Aggro](/tactics/glossary/#bad-aggro), [Chain Aggro](/tactics/glossary/#chain-aggro) and [Corpse Aggro](/tactics/glossary/#corpse-aggro) work.
- If you get aggro, do not bring it back to the team.
- You can lose aggro by dying, preferably in a spot where you are easy to rez.
- You can enable chain aggro and corpse aggro circles on [GWToolbox's minimap](https://www.gwtoolbox.com/docs/minimap/) to help you avoid delicate situations.

## Looting

Sometimes, a valuable drop will land somewhere that requires you to go out of your way to pick it up.
👉 If grabbing it risks slowing down the run, leave it.

Remember: you’re part of a team. Every extra second added to the run wastes the time of all 8 players, not just yours.

The money you “lose” by skipping a drop will be more than compensated by finishing faster, starting another run sooner, and opening more chests. 😉

### Chests

Don’t rush when picking up chests.
In City and Veil, let the Emo and the Tank take them first as they need to quickly reach the next area.

### Drop-sharing rules

At the end of the run, unassigned drops are usually free-for-all: first come, first served.

Exception: chest drops
If someone had to leave their chest behind after opening it, the gems still belong to them and should not be taken by anyone else.

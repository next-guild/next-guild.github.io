## Notes

- Use [Seed of Life] on Emo in Foundry room 4, wave 2; during Black Beast; before spiking each ball in City; under [Quickening Zephyr] at Jadoth; and before clover. During 360, use it on whoever is walling (Ranger or Emo).

- Use [Mantra of Resolve] when interrupts are possible, such as against Fiends in Veil, so you can safely deal your off-damage.

- Ping for snakes 1 and 2.

- Handle ranger hill.

- Join the glitch spot.

- Kill right-side clover and the elementalist patrol with Caller and Emo.

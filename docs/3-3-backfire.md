## Notes

- It is Required to understand how to move to the glitch spot (see video below).
- Do not take the room 5 quest in foundry, more information [here](/tactics/recoveries#failed-foundry-quest).
- [Backfire] is powerful but expensive - use it on the Dervish lord in Veil, on Sus on the wall in City, on out of ball Dryders or Kis in foundry and on out of ball Heart tormentors in gloom.
- Avoid targeting warriors with your [Energy Surge] as they can run out of energy, reducing the damage.

# MT

Similar to 3-3 MT except for the necro lord which needs a simple pull and 6-0 where you help TK with back tendrils.

## Notes

- There are many ways to bring the necro lord to the middle of the trench, usually:
  - When rangers are in front, pop [Shadow form] and [I am unstoppable!] and go [Heart of Shadow] the lord - Swapping to a low health/armor set is recommended to die faster.
  - When rangers are not here, simply bow the lord with [Recall] on a [con]Ghost-in-the-Box[/con] (See video below).
  - You can also use [Recall] to lose aggro (See video below).
  - Avoid using [Dark escape] or sidestep if you do so as any speed boost will make you lose the lord aggro.
- During 6-0, pay attention to a potential [Share Aggro](/tactics/glossary/#share-aggro) with the IAU.

# MT

Controls trench pulls and pulls the first 360 waves; the 360 start is a team decision.

## Build

[MT;OwFkUldzHPm0BkODNpNJuDCUDUnD]

## Notes

- In City Split, do not put [Recall] on Emo. After the inner spike, put [Recall] on IAU to enter Veil quickly with either TK for one 360 wave and low resurrection-scroll use, or Caller and IAU for two waves and high resurrection-scroll use.
- Ranger hill is harder without a seed. Consider holding [Death's Charge] for a ranger rather than using it early, so you can regain distance from the mobs.
- After ranger lord, place [Recall] on TK and position near the right-side clover-hill entrance. Ping [Shadow Form] when ready to start the mesmer-lord pull. Pull the necromancer lord toward the hill by moving through the necro trench, then [Recall] out to someone on the hill to move toward 6-0.

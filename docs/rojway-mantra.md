## Notes

- You are the caller even though you handle off-damage: you are the only spiker who stays with the team at all times.
- For a single wide ball such as Jadoth, take the right side while IAU takes the left. Use the same right/left assignment when several off-damage groups need attention.
- Use [Tryptophan Signet] only when Titans are present: room 3 wave 2, room 4 wave 4, and Black Beast wave 2.
- [Mantra of Resolve] is useful against Fiends at 360, clover, and 6-0. It also helps against Kayas in City and Dreamies in Foundry.
- Tank the last 360 wave. [Mantra of Resolve] lets you keep casting in front of the Fiends.
- If MLK is late at 6-0, do both the MLK and VoR tendrils. [Arcane Echo] + [Ray of Judgment] to use it on both. After your [Ray of Judgment] and [Symbol of Wrath], Seeder and Emo can auto-attack to finish the MLK tendril; Seeder can use [Ebon Battle Standard of Wisdom] to help your cooldowns.

- On City wall, each spiker casts one AoE on each mob. Bring the three middle Manks to 50% health without wasting more AoE on them; kill the first and last Manks. MLK starts with [Ray of Judgment] on the first Su and [You Move Like a Dwarf!] plus [Wastrel's Demise] on the first Mank.

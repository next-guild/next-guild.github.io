# DoA Overview

Domain of Anguish is a multi-area elite zone built around coordinated pulls and clean spikes.

## What is DoA?

- Four main areas: City, Veil, Gloom, and Foundry.
- Each area has quest objectives that must be completed before the final encounter.
- Most tactics share the same fundamentals, then differ by split pattern, build details, and role timing.

## Area overview

![Domain of Anguish map](/img/doa-callouts.jpg)

| Area | Goal | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| City | Clear the city of every margonite. Kill Jadoth. | [Bad aggros](/tactics/glossary/#bad-aggro). |
| Veil | Clear waves of enemies during 360. Unlock trenches by slaying the dervish and ranger lords. Kill the 4 trench lords. Kill tendrils and perform a final spike in one (6-0) or two batches (3-3). | Back aggros & trench triggers. |
| Gloom | Secure cave, cap the rift, spike the deathbringer ball and finish with darknesses. | Don't let earth tormentors through during waves. Synchronized kills on darknesses. |
| Foundry | Advance in multiple rooms, protect snakes up to the black beast. Kill the fury. | Stuck snakes. [Cast range](/tactics/fundamentals/#cast-range) during fury. |

# 5 Man

5 Man is a compact advanced tactic built around a Necro SS, TK, MeMo, Emo, and a single Tank.

## Roles

| Role | Focus | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SS | Necro spike support with [Spiteful Spirit], [Mark of Pain], spirits, and utility. | [Open role](/tactics/5man/ss) |
| TK | Handles gloom quests, snakes, off-damage, city hill setup, derv hill and tendrils. | [Open role](/tactics/5man/tk) |
| MeMo | Provides [Healing Seed], extra mesmer spike, and derv hill support. | [Open role](/tactics/5man/memo) |
| Emo | Bonds, rift, and team maintenance. | [Open role](/tactics/5man/emo) |
| Tank | Main tank for the run. | [Open role](/tactics/5man/tank) |

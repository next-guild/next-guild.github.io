# MeMo

MeMo replaces UA. It adds more spiking power, but the team loses UA survivability and convenient [Seed of Life] range.

## Build

[MeMo;OQNDAqwNS0lLBnAmO4UTP2kSEA]

## Gear

Use the shared [Mesmer gear](/tactics/gear/mesmer) page as the baseline.

## Notes

- Use [Healing Seed] at the timing where UA would normally use [Seed of Life].
- [Healing Seed] has much smaller range, so place it directly on the tank more often.
- In foundry room 4, help kill wave 3 and 4 on spawn instead of waiting for TT to make clean balls.
- You can use [Healing Seed] on IAU before he pulls the Black Beast of Aaaaarrrrrrggghhh.
- During clover, put [Healing Seed] directly on MT.
- During fast wall, finish the last mank with MLK while VoR, IAU, and TK leave early.
- During the city inner split, spike left side with VoR and IAU.
- You're in charge of performing the recall tactic in gloom at TT cave in place of the IAU.

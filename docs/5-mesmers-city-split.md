# 5 Mez City Split

5 Mez City Split replaces the UA with a MeMo. The run gains more spiking power allowing for more splits, but loses some survivability and quality of life.

:::info Work in progress
Gameplay videos are missing and will be added soon.
:::

## What changes from 6-0

- MeMo uses [Healing Seed] where UA would normally use [Seed of Life].
- [Healing Seed] has much smaller range than [Seed of Life], so it often needs to be placed directly on the tank.
- VoR is now in charge of placing [Edge of extinction] about the same locations as the UA would.
- Foundry room 4 wave 3 and 4 are killed more aggressively, without waiting for the TT to make perfect balls.
- The added mesmer damage allows the team to spike both city inner balls at the same time.
- The first two waves at 360 require often some [con]Scroll of resurrection[/con].

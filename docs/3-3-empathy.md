# Empathy

Main ball spiker with a focus on [C-target](/tactics/glossary#c-target) and single target damage thanks to [Empathy] and [Spiritual pain].

## Notes

- You are in charge of [C-target](/tactics/glossary#c-target), call for help when there are too many of them.
- Don't use [Empathy] on Manks while doing City wall as it will reduce [Visions of regret] damage, [Energy Surge], [Unnatural Signet] and [Spiritual pain] will be more optimal.
- [Empathy] is a powerful tool to deal with Jadoth in city or off-ball melees in foundry such as Ruk/Vu/Dementia.
- Leave the necromancer lord when he is at about 50% health after casting a last [Empathy] on him.
- Avoid targeting warriors with your [Energy Surge] as they can run out of energy, reducing the damage.

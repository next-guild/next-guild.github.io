# TT

Handles City transitions and the monk-lord pull without spawning the Gloom trench.

## Build

[TT;OwFkUld3HPa0BkODNZozEECEuTnD]

## Notes

- In City Split, put [Recall] on Emo after the inner spike, then [Recall] out and use [Death's Charge] to start Jadoth.
- Do not spawn the Gloom trench or bring monk-trench groups to MLK. Use [Shadow of Haste] deep, pull the mobs away, and [Dash] out. MLK glitches the mob to make room for your monk-lord pull.
- Always exit Backway.

## Notes

- Lead with [Deep Freeze] in City. Elsewhere, lead with [Visions of Regret].

- You are the caller. Be vocal and lead the team, including calling when to spike so teammates do not cast early. These tactics especially benefit from clear leadership while people get used to the bars.

- Put [Chaos Storm] on IAU's front tendril to help with damage. This is required.

- On City wall, use [Visions of Regret] on every Mank.

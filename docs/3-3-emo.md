# Emo

Makes the life of everyone easier by reducing damage and providing support.

## Build

[Emo;OgNDwcPPTaR3MkE1C0lyDxDHEA]

## Gear

Use the shared [Emo gear](/tactics/gear/emo) page as the baseline.

## Notes

- You can maintain up to 14 maximum permanent bonds on your team.
- Cover bonds with [Balthazar's Spirit] and [Spirit bond] in foundry when facing groups containing Despair titans who have [Chilblains] and dream riders who have [Mirror of disenchantment] and [Shatter enchantment].
- Watch your MT's position at all times when he has [Recall] on you.
- Ask for help from your UA when needing more energy from [Ether renewal].
- Drop your [Protective bond] when tanking the 3-3 when running low on energy.
- You are in charge of tanking the first two waves and fifth wave of 360.

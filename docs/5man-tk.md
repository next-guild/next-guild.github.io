## Notes

### Gloom

- Keep [Recall] on the tank.
- Recall out as soon as the tank climbs the hill, then go to cave using [Heart of Shadow].
- Spike off-damage during the last spike of waves.
- Take the quest in cave and at Gloom start.
- Go back to Darknesses to help for wave 3.

### Foundry

- Take all snakes.
- Recall out from the third snake immediately to avoid getting [Mirror of disenchantment].
- You can ask Emo to remove your bonds before the third snake so the tank does not risk losing them.
- Aggro Black Beast of Aaaaarrrrrrggghhh.

### City

- Focus on off-damage.
- Kill Sus on the wall.
- Delay the right-side patrol in inner city by recalling the Necro and running up the hill. Manks will be in aggro but casters will not, avoiding [Recall] getting stripped.
- Take the Jadoth quest.

### Veil

- Pull left hill during 360.
- Go make derv hill with MeMo. [Recall] him, [Arcane echo] your [Heart of Shadow]. (See video below)
- Spike the groups the tank brings to the glitch spot.
- If the tank managed to pull the left-side clover group, go to Mesmer Lord; otherwise go to Monk Lord.
- Echo [Pain Inverter] the two back tendrils during the first 3-3.
- Single [Pain Inverter] the farthest tendril on the second 3-3.

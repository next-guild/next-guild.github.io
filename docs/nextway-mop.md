## Notes

- Use [Earthbind] on difficult spikes. Call [Mark of Pain] on knocked-down foes and watch off-damage before committing.
- Apply [You Move Like a Dwarf!] plus [Necrosis] to C-targets before [Mark of Pain]. Place [Spiteful Spirit] early on Fiends during 360.
- In City Split, spike left side. In Veil Split, spike the left-side clover patrol from hilltop with TK and Caller, then summon a minion for TK to put [Recall] on and fetch IAU, Emo, and MT returning from ranger hill. Move with Caller to right-side clover hill to glitch the patrol before the team spikes the pulled lords.
- Do the usual VoR tendril in 6-0 and give [Ebon Escape] to Caller.
- In Gloom, move toward the start after cave spike for TK and TT’s [Recall] chain, and watch for IAU using [Recall] toward an early Deathbringer.

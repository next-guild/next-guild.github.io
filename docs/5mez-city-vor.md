# VoR

You are in charge of the [Edge of extinction] similarly to how the UA would place it in 6-0.

## Build

[VoR;OQJTASBbTiu0tZDQwx0Z6JmE6AA]

## Gear

Use the shared [Mesmer gear](/tactics/gear/mesmer) page as the baseline.

## Notes

- In foundry room 4, help kill wave 3 and 4 on spawn instead of waiting for TT to make clean balls. Feel free to use a [con]Tengu Support Flare[/con] to help.
- Try to place [Edge of Extinction] both in a [Quickening Zephyr] and in range of the first Su and Mank of the wall for 1st outer.
- During fast wall, leave early with IAU and TK to spike second outer.
- During the city inner split, spike left side with TT, MeMo, Emo, and IAU.
- Place [Edge of Extinction] for Jadoth immediately as you enter [Quickening Zephyr]. Let TK copy again your [Visions of Regret].
- Go with MLK and MT for the first two waves of 360. Bring resurrection scrolls for this section.

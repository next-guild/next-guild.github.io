# Spike Rework Notes

These notes describe the current post-patch spike ideas and area-by-area responsibilities.

## Spike Types

### Knock Spike

Use:

- [Intensity]
- [Dragon's Stomp]

An intensified [Dragon's Stomp] secures the IAU, prevents deballing and healing, and puts every nearby foe below 90%.

### Double Knock Spike

Use:

1. [Dragon's Stomp]
2. [Earthquake]
3. [Intensity]
4. [Rodgort's Invocation]
5. [Fireball]

Use this on more difficult spikes. It keeps foes knocked down for longer, and [Intensity] on [Rodgort's Invocation] is massive damage, often 250+.

### No-Knock Spike

Use:

- [Intensity]
- [Rodgort's Invocation]
- [Fireball]

Use any suitable spike.

- [Mistrust].

### Split

MT side:

- Caller + IAU.
- Double knock spike.

TT side:

- Everyone else.

### 360 and Jadoth

Caller + IAU go to 360 and use double knock spike. This should save some scrolls.

Everyone else goes to Jadoth and uses the regular VoR / Energy Surge spike.

360 should be easy: Caller + TK can focus on off-damage, because [Mark of Pain] + IAU is enough to wipe main.

## Veil Split

### TT Side

TT + MLK go to derv hill.

Do derv hill as usual:

- No Gloom trench pop.
- No glitch spot before Monk Lord.
- MLK can cast Signet on the incoming patrol to keep enemies in the trench and give TT space.

### Ranger Hill and Clover

- TK goes to ranger hill with MT.
- Caller + MoP go to left clover hill.
- Emo stands between both sides to keep bonds up on everyone.

### Left Clover

IAU recalls Minion once left clover spike is dead.

Use:

- Double knock spike.
- Energy Surge / [Mistrust] / Energy Surge.

If Ranger or Dervish takes a while, MoP or Caller can heal Minion with [Ebon Escape].

### Transition to Right Clover

IAU goes toward turtle trench entrance.

MT recalls IAU.

Emo triggers the recall chain with [Ebon Escape].

Without scripts, the other option is to [Ebon Escape] down the hill on MT after the recall chain.

Caller + MoP + TK transition to right clover hill.

Emo + IAU do Ele Lord.

Main team glitches right clover and spikes the Mesmer Lord and Necro Lord pulled by MT.

### Back to 6-0

Everyone goes back to 6-0 using recalls and [Ebon Escape].

For tendrils:

- IAU places [Pain Inverter] on back IAU tendril.
- IAU + Caller spike front IAU tendril.
- Caller can send one spell on back IAU tendril.
- MLK and MoP each do one tendril.
- TK does the usual job.

## 6-0

Caller can start double knocking early to prevent C-targets.

Spike plan:

- All mesmers on fiends.
- MoP + IAU spike main once off-damage is ready.

## Gloom

### Deathbringer

### Darknesses

## Notes

- Call knockdowns for difficult spikes: Foundry room 3 wave 2, Fury, and City balls. [Obsidian Flame] helps with wall clear and C-target management.
- In City Split, spike right side and take the Jadoth quest. You may start the first two 360 waves with IAU and MT.
- In Veil Split, spike the left-side clover patrol from hilltop with TK and MoP. After TK fetches IAU, Emo, and MT, move with MoP to right-side clover hill and glitch the patrol before the team spikes the pulled lords.
- Stack with IAU for tendrils and use [Ebon Escape] on MoP to exit.
- In Gloom, leave after wave 4 after placing [Edge of Extinction] for cave spike, then use it again to cover for an early Deathbringer and cap the rift with Emo.

Use this section for focused techniques that are too specific for the run overview pages.

## Fast wall in city

- First Mank and Su should be killed by the [Edge of extinction] during the first outer spike due to the Backfire/MLK setting them up.
- Every mesmer skips the 3 middle Manks except for the VoR who spikes them with [Visions of regret] and [Unnatural Signet].
- The [Edge of extinction] needs to be placed close to the wall to not be in line of sight of the Manks.

![EoE placement map](/img/fast-wall-eoe.png)

- The last Mank should be killed entirely as it will be out of [Edge of extinction] range.
- The main tank needs to do a fast ball to be in time for the spike as this way of clearing the wall doesn't leave enough time for the regular ball.

## Bondless elementalist lord

- Place [Pain Inverter] on the lord.
- [Recall] out when he starts casting [Sandstorm].
- Pop a [con]Imperial Guard Reinforcement Order[/con] and wait until you're dead.
- Wait for someone to revive you.

## Pulling Jadoth

- The Veil punishes blocking and evading because each trigger causes 50 damage.
- Therefore, don’t dodge projectiles during pull.
- Use low HP gear so your percentage-based thresholds line up with damage reduction math.
- If your [Shroud of distress] isn’t doubled - damage reduction of 15 - stay around 300 HP.
- If you doubled your [Shroud of distress] - damage reduction of 20 - you can go closer to 400 HP+.
- [Seed of life] support is unreliable, so the build should survive periods without constant healing.
- Only source of damage is burning from [Anthem of Flame], not direct enemy hits.
- Ensure you don't fall below 50% HP as [Shroud of Distress] will trigger the veil effect.

:::info
What does doubling the shroud mean ?

While under [Shadow form],
The -5 damage reduction is active for the expected duration of each enchantment, not its actual duration. Refreshing an Assassin enchantment before it ends reduces the received damage by an additional -5.
:::

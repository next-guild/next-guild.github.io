# MeMo

MeMo provides [Healing Seed], mesmer damage, and support for derv hill.

## Build

[MeMo;OQNEAqwN+ioButpL4bw0LRxD]

## Gear

Use the shared [Mesmer gear](/tactics/gear/mesmer) page as the baseline.

## Notes

[Life Bond] is only here to provide infinite energy between Foundry room 3 and Veil. Cast it on Emo, and Emo places [Balthazar's Spirit] on you.

### Gloom

- Wait with Necro before triggering the mobs at the start.
- Go with Emo in Gloom to cap the rift after wave 8.
- Help on Darknesses wave 3.

### Foundry

- Use [Healing Seed] on Emo in Foundry room 4 wave 2.
- Ping for snakes until General is free, then leave it to Emo.
- Try to seed TK before the Black Beast pull.

### City

- Place [Visions of regret] on the main ball before hitting off-damage.
- Help with everything on the wall.

### Veil

- Seed during 360: Emo during melee waves, tank otherwise.
- [Life Bond] TK before the derv hill pull.
- Go with the tank to kill Monk Lord.
- Join back your team at Mesmer Lord.
- Seed Emo for 3-3.
- Kill the closest tendril during the first 3-3 spike and the middle tendril during the second one.

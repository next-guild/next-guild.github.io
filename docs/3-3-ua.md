# UA

Massive healing potential with [Seed of life], quick resurrections with [Unyielding aura] and spike enabler through [Edge of extinction].

## Build

[UA;OwIT8MIbX6uKHUuE6gukhAg4BEA]

When playing with [con]Lunar Fortune[/con], reduce Divine Favor to 11 and increase Beast Mastery to 12.

## Gear

Use the shared [UA gear](/tactics/gear/ua) page as the baseline.

## Notes
- [Edge of extinction] is absolutely crucial to place before big spikes, warn the team if it's on cooldown.
- [Shield of absorption] is not instant, your target needs to receive a few hits before zeroing damage.
- When your emo needs energy, you can use [Shield of absorption] and [Aura of Stability] on him to help him out.
- It's often interesting to use [Aura of Stability] on the [Visions of regret] mesmer before spikes with enemies with [Meteor] or [Gale].
- On places with huge death potential like foundry, keep [Unyielding aura] up at all times to allow for double rez.
- [Pain Inverter] is here to help clear the wall and do off-ball damage otherwise. Call it when you use it in such a way so your target doesn't get cancelled with [Mistrust] or [You Move Like a Dwarf!].
- [Pain Inverter] is also used to clear a claw of torment to cap the rift in Gloom, stay in melee range of it to make it attack you.
- You are in charge of tanking the last wave of 360.

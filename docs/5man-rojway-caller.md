# Caller

Mesmer caller with [Visions of Regret], [Edge of Extinction], and [Winnowing].

## Build

[Caller;OQJUIYQaT8QuNTP3U4U2kQHPH0l]

## Gear

Use the regular [Mesmer gear](/tactics/gear/mesmer) and the pcons listed there.

## Notes

- Call the spike in Foundry room 3, wave 2.

- Use [Winnowing] in Gloom, Foundry room 5, and City, wherever the Ranger uses [Whirling Defense].

- Elsewhere, use only [Edge of Extinction].

- Handle derv hill.

- At the glitch spot, place [Edge of Extinction].

- Kill right-side clover and the elementalist patrol with Emo and Seeder.

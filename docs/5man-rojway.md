# 5man Rojway

A five-player variant with a Ranger Tank, Mesmer Caller, Smiter TK, Seeder, and Emo.

## Roles

| Role | Focus | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tank | Main tank for the run. | [Open role](/tactics/5man-rojway/tank) |
| Caller | Mesmer caller with [Visions of Regret], [Edge of Extinction], and [Winnowing]. | [Open role](/tactics/5man-rojway/caller) |
| TK | Smiter Monk with [Ray of Judgment], [Pain Inverter], and [Recall]. | [Open role](/tactics/5man-rojway/tk) |
| Seeder | Supports the team with [Seed of Life] and Smiting damage. | [Open role](/tactics/5man-rojway/seeder) |
| Emo | Maintains bonds with the same build as 5man Mezway. | [Open role](/tactics/5man-rojway/emo) |

## Gear

- Tank: regular [Ranger gear](/tactics/gear/ranger), as in 5man Mezway.

- Caller: regular [Mesmer gear](/tactics/gear/mesmer).

- TK and Seeder: regular [Smiter gear](/tactics/gear/smiting-monk). Seeder also needs an additional +3 Divine Favor armor piece.

- Emo: the same build as [5man Mezway Emo](/tactics/5man/emo), with regular [Emo gear](/tactics/gear/emo).

## Notes

- You are the main source of damage, carefully respect [Cast Range](/tactics/fundamentals/#cast-range) principles as other mesmers need your [Visions of regret] to enable their [Unnatural Signet].
- You call the spikes for the rest of the team.
- Pay attention to the presence of [Edge of extinction].
- Delay your spike if off-damage seems difficult to pull off in time.
- Be comfortable with retargeting as you will sometimes need to [Shatter Delusions] twice on a same spike.

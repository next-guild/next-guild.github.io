# Nextway

Nextway is a 6-0 variant built around a [Vow of Strength] IAU, a [Mark of Pain] MoP, and a Caller; it replaces UA and VoR. It has a standard route, plus City Split and Veil Split variants.

## Roles

| Role | Focus | Page |

## Variants

### City Split

- TK and MoP spike left-side City. Caller spikes the right side and takes the Jadoth quest.
- TT puts [Recall] on Emo after the inner spike, then uses [Recall] to leave, seals, and uses [Death's Charge] to start Jadoth.
- MT does not put [Recall] on Emo. After the inner spike, MT puts [Recall] on IAU to enter Veil quickly.

### Veil Split

- After 360, MLK and TT do the dervish and monk lords without the four-wave spike.
- Caller, MoP, and TK go to left-side clover hill and spike the patrol from hilltop. Emo, MT, and IAU kill the ranger lord.
- Once the patrol and ranger lord are dead, MoP summons a minion. TK puts [Recall] on it and fetches IAU, Emo, and MT returning from ranger hill.
- Caller and MoP move to right-side clover hill and glitch the right-side clover patrol.
- MT calls the [Recall] chain off. IAU and Emo kill the elementalist lord together while the rest of the team spikes the mesmer and necromancer lords from right-side clover hill.

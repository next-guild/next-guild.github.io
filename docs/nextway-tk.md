# TK

Handles [You Move Like a Dwarf!] targets, regular tendrils, and the Veil Split setup.

## Build

[TK;OQdSAYBTLBnAmOTP3Uyl2kdO]

## Notes

- If the team agrees, skip the Fury spike to put [Recall] on the City quest snake so TT can quick-start City.
- In City Split, spike left side and put [Recall] on Caller to give MT a jump. You may clear 360 wave 1 with MT.
- In Veil Split, spike the left-side clover patrol from hilltop with Caller and MoP. After MoP summons a minion, put [Recall] on it and fetch IAU, Emo, and MT returning from ranger hill. Caller and MoP glitch the right-side clover patrol while the team spikes the mesmer and necromancer lords from hilltop.
- Do the regular 6-0 tendrils.
- In Gloom, put [Recall] on MoP and give TT a [Recall] chain after cave spike.

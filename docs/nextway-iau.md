## Notes

- Use [I Am Unstoppable!] before spikes with knockdowns, such as [Gale] in City. Spike when the ritualist calls [Mark of Pain], but check off-damage first.
- In Foundry room 3 wave 2, put [Recall] on Emo before the spike and wait for a safe knockdown window so it is not stripped.
- For the first two 360 waves, pull the waves that TT would usually handle with Caller and MT.
- In the Veil Split, kill the ranger lord with Emo and MT (You can use [con]Imperial Guard Reinforcement Order[/con] if needed). 
- After the [Recall] chain is called off, kill the elementalist lord with Emo: [Death's Charge] the farthest ele-patrol mob, use [Pain Inverter], and use [Recall] to leave when the lord starts [Sandstorm]. [Example video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mgu6bqaLB34&t=849s)
- During 6-0, do the usual IAU tendrils with Caller and use [Pain Inverter] on the farthest tendril.
- Exit Gloom through Goltway after 6-0. Put [Recall] on a backliner and hold the Gloom spawn briefly.
- In Gloom, help with waves and cave spike, then leave with MLK to watch for an early Deathbringer. You may lag behind to give TK a [Recall] target after placing [Recall] on MLK.

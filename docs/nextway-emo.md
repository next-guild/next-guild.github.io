## Notes

- In City Split, once TT pulls down, start walking outside to give TT good positioning to start the Jadoth pull.
- In Veil Split, join IAU and MT at ranger hill. After MT calls the [Recall] chain off, trigger the chain by using [Ebon Escape] on the TK.
- Kill the elementalist lord with IAU; use [con]Imperial Guard Reinforcement Order[/con] to maximize [Pain Inverter] damage. [Example video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mgu6bqaLB34&t=849s)

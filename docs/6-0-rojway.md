# 6-0 Rojway

A [regular 6-0](/tactics/4-mesmers-6-0) variant using [Ray of Judgment] Smiting Monks. Mantra calls spikes while handling off-damage. MT, TT, and Emo follow regular 6-0 with the builds below.

## Gear and consumables

Mantra, IAU, and MLK use [Smiter gear](/tactics/gear/smiting-monk), with the same pcons as mesmers. TK uses Mesmer gear and UA uses UA gear; MT, TT, and Emo keep their usual gear and pcons. Role-specific title requirements are listed on each role page.

## City wall

On City wall, each spiker casts one AoE on each mob. Bring the three middle Manks to 50% health without wasting more AoE on them; kill the first and last Manks. MLK starts with [Ray of Judgment] on the first Su and [You Move Like a Dwarf!] plus [Wastrel's Demise] on the first Mank.

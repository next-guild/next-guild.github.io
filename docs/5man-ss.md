# SS

Necro damage and utility role for 5 Man.

## Build

[SS;OAJUYsDbXsQWC5BuQwBQHPH2kxkA]

_You can also use [Necrosis] instead of [Finish him!]._

## Gear

Use the shared [Necro gear](/tactics/gear/necro) page as the baseline.

## Notes

### Gloom

- Wait for TK to be close to aggroing the first group before triggering the Gloom mobs.
- Wait for the Water Tormentor to enter the Whisper Agents range, then cast [You move like a dwarf!] on Mind Freeze if needed.
- Place [Winnowing] and [Edge of extinction] in range of waves.
- Kill the main ball with [Mark of Pain].
- Provide TK with a [Recall] target after cave is completed.
- Place [Winnowing] and [Edge of extinction] in range of the Deathbringer.
- Place spirits again in range of the Darknesses kill spot.
- After ranger clears the five Darknesses, spike Earth Tormentors with [Desecrate enchantments] and [Defile enchantments].

### Foundry

- Place [Edge of extinction] in rooms 1, 3, and 4.
- Use [You move like a dwarf!] on Ki and Dream Riders.
- Place [Spiteful Spirit] on Vu, Rund, Dementia, and Dryders.
- Knock the Touch of Aaaaarrrrrrggghhh.
- Place [Winnowing] and [Edge of extinction] to help the ranger kill the Fury ball.

### City

- Use [Mark of Pain] on every spike.
- During wall clear, focus on Manks with [Spiteful Spirit] and [Finish him!] or [Necrosis].
- Place [Edge of extinction] before every spike.
- Give [Heart of Shadow] to TK so he can climb the hill faster to pull the right-side inner group.

### Veil

- Place [Edge of extinction] and spike 360 with [Spiteful Spirit], [Desecrate enchantments], and [Defile enchantments].
- Kill Ranger Lord with the tank. You should be able to clear it with [Spiteful Spirit], auto-attacks, and [Necrosis] or [Finish him!]. Use a [con]Tengu Support Flare[/con] if needed.
- Place [Edge of extinction] in the Monk Lord glitch spot.
- If the tank managed to pull the left-side clover group, go to Mesmer Lord; otherwise go to Monk Lord.
- Place one [Spiteful Spirit] on the Necro Lord and run away.
- During 3-3, you are responsible for killing the first tendril of the second three-tendril kill.

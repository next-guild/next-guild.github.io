# TT

Secondary tank in charge of splits.

## Build

[TT;OwFkMOd5HXlENpODuDCUDUozBUnD]

Experienced TT sometimes use [Dash] instead of [Dark escape], trading survability for speed.

## Gear

Use the shared [TT gear](/tactics/gear/tt) page as the baseline.

## Notes

- Cave is the only place where you can fail the run, practice it beforehand (see video below).
- The veil area is the most tricky one with the Dervish & Monk lords followed by challenging glitches with [Shadow of Haste], there are ways to practice it solo as well (see video below).
- Be careful of [Famine] in foundry while holding enemies away from the snake.
- The monk lord has many different spawns, check out the [TT Monk lord](/tactics/in-depth-concepts#pulling-the-monk-lord-as-tt) in depth concept to understand how to handle each one.


It can be interesting to understand how Dream riders movement work when balling the Fury without [Shadow of Haste], here is a little paint with the dreamies in blue and the dryders in red:
![Fury no SoH](/img/fury-no-soh.jpg)
The core idea is to go in between the two groups and then move slowly until the dreamies are on top of the dryders.

Main tank for 5 Man.

## Notes

### Gloom

- [Recall] emo, [Heart of Shadow] up the cliff, go take wave 1.
- Clear path for TK by aggroing cave mobs. Kill rupts (Sanity, Spirit) first.
- Once wave 9-12 begin spawning, pull everything towards wave 12 and start making a ball.
- [Whirling defense] to put wave 12 below EoE threshold then [Death's charge] inside the main ball.
- Recall out to emo, place a new recall, ping [Shadow form] when ready, pull everything towards the wall between rift and deathbringer, whirl down the melees.
- Wait for the ball to settle, whirl down the deathbringer.
- Whirl first darknesses on spawn, then pull the second wave of darknesses towards the wall before using [Death's charge].

### Foundry

- Use [Shadow form] in room 1.
- Make balls in room 2 if the team doesn't have aggro. [Recall] your emo before entering room 3.
- Aggro the dryders first in room 3 and pull the rest of the enemies.
- Room 4 - no change compared to regular MT/TT job.
- Room 5 - regular MT job, clear 3rd snake after the 2 first snakes are taken.
- Whirl down the fury using a right wall block.

### City

- Don't use [Dwarven Stability] as your [Whirling defense] would last for too long.

### Veil

- Sidewalk if you have a cupcake at ranger hill.
- Run into mesmer trench and pull the back groups towards the glitch spot, try to grab the left-side clover group too.
- Feel free to [Recall] and pull away the patrol for your Memo to pull the monk lord.

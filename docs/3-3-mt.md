# MT

Main tank running alongside the spikers.

## Build

[MT;OwFkUld5HPOENpOTDECEujN5BUnD]

Experienced MT sometimes use [Dash] instead of [Dark escape], trading survability for speed.

## Gear

Use the shared [MT gear](/tactics/gear/mt) page as the baseline.

## Notes

- Take your time in City to avoid [Bad Aggro](/tactics/glossary#bad-aggro).
- Take a look at [why you need to swap to a low health armor to pull Jadoth](/tactics/in-depth-concepts#pulling-jadoth).
- Make smart use of enemies [Quickening Zephyr] to perma [I am unstoppable!] in City and Foundry.
- Don't use [Shroud of Distress] in Veil.
- Watch your emo's position at all times when you have [Recall] on him.

![City patrols map](/img/city-patrols.png)
You usually want to start the 2nd outer city ball with a [Death's Charge] on the red patrol and work your way up from there.
Do not hesitate to use [Heart of Shadow] to instantly take aggro of a moving enemy group instead of walking up to an enemy ball to reduce the chance of getting [Bad Aggro](/tactics/glossary#bad-aggro).

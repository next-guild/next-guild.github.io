## Notes

- Feel free to ask for help if the off-damage is too difficult to pull off in time. 
- On big spikes, a single [Energy surge] on the off-damage is enough - you only want to put mobs under 90% for [Edge of extinction].
- On smaller spikes like during the 360, you want to keep spiking the off-damage as [Edge of extinction] will proc less.
- Wait patiently for the dervish lord to break aggro from the TT and avoid share aggro when pulling with your bow.
- Do not use [Mistrust] on the ele lord before he used his [Sandstorm] under your [Pain Inverter].
- Kill the tendrils in order (farthest one first) to ease the main tank pull.
- You can use your [Recall] to break or delay enemies as when [saving the first snake](/tactics/in-depth-concepts#safing-first-snake-as-tk) in foundry.
- Since you have [Recall], pick up your chests last.
- Avoid targeting warriors with your [Energy Surge] as they can run out of energy, reducing the damage.
